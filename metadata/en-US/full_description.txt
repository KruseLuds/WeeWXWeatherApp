Unlike general weather apps, which get data from third party websites, this app gets data from personal weather stations running WeeWX.

It's a requirement that you need to install the AggregateValues and InigoPlugin extensions in WeeWX, and optionally the MQTTPublishV2 extension.
Those extensions are needed because they facilitate exporting data from WeeWX to this app, more details are available at https://github.com/evilbunny2008/WeeWXWeatherApp/wiki
